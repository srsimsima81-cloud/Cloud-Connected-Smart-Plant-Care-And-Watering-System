# Industry Relevance

## Use cases
- Smart agriculture: field telemetry and irrigation decisions.
- Precision farming: data-driven irrigation rather than fixed schedules.
- Greenhouses: centralized environmental monitoring and actuator control.
- Nurseries: many plants/devices monitored from one dashboard.
- Smart homes: indoor plant automation.
- Vertical farming/hydroponics: centralized telemetry, rules and alerts.
- Commercial landscaping: remote fleet/device monitoring.
- Environmental monitoring: sensor data collection, history and anomaly alerts.

## Business benefits demonstrated
- reduced water wastage through threshold-based watering
- remote monitoring from a browser
- historical analytics for trends
- early alerts for dry soil/high temperature/low tank/device offline
- centralized management of multiple devices
- scalable cloud architecture for additional plants
